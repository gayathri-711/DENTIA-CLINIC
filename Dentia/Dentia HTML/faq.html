Book Appointment

You can book directly through our Online Booking Engine. Simply select your desired medical department (e.g. Cardiology, Orthopedics, Pediatrics), choose your attending doctor, view real-time available time slots, and submit. You will receive an immediate confirmation reference (e.g. MED-2026-XXXX).

Call our 24/7 Emergency Line immediately at +1 (800) 555-9111 or dispatch a mobile ICU ambulance at +1 (800) 555-9199. Our Level-I Trauma Center operates continuously without interruption 365 days a year.

We accept all major health insurance networks, including BlueCross BlueShield, Aetna, Cigna, UnitedHealthcare, Medicare, and regional HMO networks. Our dedicated Cashier & Insurance Claims Desk in the main atrium handles pre-authorization and direct billing.

All patient records, e-prescriptions, and laboratory values are securely accessible 24/7 through the Patient Health Portal. Sign in with your registered email to view clinical encounters, download PDF test reports, and view paid invoices.

Inpatient visiting hours are daily from 04:00 PM to 07:00 PM. Intensive Care Units (ICU/CCU/NICU) have restricted visiting hours (11:00 AM - 12:00 PM and 05:00 PM - 06:00 PM) with one visitor permitted at a time to maintain sterile patient safety.
