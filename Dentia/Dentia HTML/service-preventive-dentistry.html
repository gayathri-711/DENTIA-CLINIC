Clinical Procedure Overview

Nothing lights up a room like a brilliant, radiant white smile. Our professional in-office laser teeth whitening system safely and effectively lifts years of deep-set stains caused by coffee, tea, red wine, dark sauces, and natural aging. Unlike over-the-counter kits that can erode enamel and cause severe sensitivity, our clinical whitening utilizes dentist-prescribed hydrogen peroxide activated by LED wavelengths for dazzling, uniform results in under an hour.

When is this Procedure Recommended?

  • Yellowed, discolored, or dull teeth needing instant brightness for weddings, interviews, or events
  • Stubborn extrinsic stains from coffee, espresso, tea, soda, or tobacco use
  • Desire for safe clinical whitening without gum burning or prolonged sensitivity
  • Preventative fluoride remineralization to protect tooth enamel post-whitening

Your Treatment Journey & What to Expect

01
Baseline Shade Assessment

We measure your starting enamel shade against our clinical VITA guide and photograph baseline.

02
Protective Gingival Barrier

A soothing medical barrier is applied over your gums and lips to guarantee total comfort.

03
Laser Activated Gel Application

Clinical-grade whitening gel applied across teeth and activated with cool blue LED light for three 15-minute intervals.

04
Desensitizing Enamel Polish

Fluoride and potassium nitrate remineralizing treatment locks in brightness and prevents sensitivity.

Key Benefits of Teeth Whitening & Preventive Care

  • Lightens teeth up to 8 shades in a single fast, comfortable 45-minute clinic session
  • Safe, pH-balanced formula engineered to protect enamel and prevent gum irritation
  • Instant results visible immediately before you leave the dental chair
  • Includes take-home touch-up guidance to maintain your pearly white sparkle for up to 2 years
