Our Foundation

Dedicated to Transforming Patient Lives for Over 20 Years

Founded with the vision to deliver world-class medical care at international benchmarks, MEDINOVA Multi-Speciality Hospital has grown into a leading tertiary referral center serving thousands of patients annually.

Our 450-bed quaternary facility integrates 12 Centers of Excellence—including Cardiovascular Sciences, Neurosciences, Robotic Orthopedics, Oncology, and Comprehensive Emergency Care. We pair internationally trained clinicians with AI-driven diagnostic imaging and precision surgical robotics to ensure the highest standard of healing.

"At MEDINOVA, our motto is 'Advanced Healthcare, Better Tomorrow.' We treat the whole human being, not just isolated symptoms, fostering trust through transparent clinical decisions and empathetic healing."

— Dr. Arthur Sterling, Chief Medical Officer

Our Vision

To be the most trusted healthcare institution globally recognized for pioneering medical therapies, clinical safety, and empathetic patient outcomes.

Our Mission

To provide accessible, ethical, and high-precision multi-speciality clinical care through continuous technological innovation and patient-first medical practice.

Healthcare Standards
