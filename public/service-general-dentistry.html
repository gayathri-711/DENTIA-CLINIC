Clinical Procedure Overview

General dentistry forms the foundational cornerstone of lifelong oral health. Our clinic provides thorough dental examinations, high-resolution digital X-rays, ultrasonic scaling, professional dental polishing, and early cavity detection. By identifying dental problems in their earliest stages, we preserve your natural teeth and prevent costly future complications.

When is this Procedure Recommended?

  • Routine bi-annual dental checkups and professional cleaning
  • Mild tooth sensitivity, minor ache, or plaque buildup
  • Halitosis (persistent bad breath) or bleeding gums during brushing
  • Preventive fissure sealants and strengthening fluoride varnish

Your Treatment Journey & What to Expect

01
Comprehensive Diagnostic Exam

Digital intraoral scan, periodontal pocket charting, and low-radiation digital radiography.

02
Ultrasonic Scaling & Plaque Removal

Painless ultrasonic vibration gently dissolves hardened calculus and bacterial biofilm.

03
Tooth Polishing & Stain Eraser

Gentle micro-abrasive polishing leaves tooth surfaces ultra-smooth and naturally bright.

04
Personalized Care Plan

Customized brushing guidance, diet recommendations, and preventative follow-up schedule.

Key Benefits of General Dentistry

  • Eliminates harmful plaque and tartar from hard-to-reach gumline areas
  • Early detection of oral cancer, enamel demineralization, and gingivitis
  • Preserves natural tooth structure, preventing extensive root canals or extractions
  • Fresh breath, polished teeth, and enhanced overall cardiovascular health
