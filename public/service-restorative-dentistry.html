Clinical Procedure Overview

Restorative dentistry restores full masticatory function, structural integrity, and aesthetic beauty to teeth damaged by trauma, deep decay, or tooth loss. Dental implants represent the gold standard of modern tooth replacement: biocompatible medical-grade titanium posts anchored in the jawbone that fuse permanently with bone (osseointegration) and support realistic porcelain crowns.

When is this Procedure Recommended?

  • One or more missing teeth causing chewing difficulty or speech issues
  • Severely fractured, broken, or heavily decayed teeth needing a crown
  • Loose, ill-fitting, or uncomfortable traditional removable dentures
  • Preventing jawbone shrinkage and facial sagging following tooth loss

Your Treatment Journey & What to Expect

01
3D Cone Beam CT (CBCT) Scan

Three-dimensional digital mapping of bone density, nerve pathways, and implant trajectory.

02
Computer-Guided Implant Placement

Minimally invasive surgical placement of titanium implant post under complete anesthesia.

03
Natural Osseointegration

Implant securely integrates with jawbone over a short healing window, forming a permanent root.

04
Custom Porcelain Crown Placement

Handcrafted zirconium or porcelain crown permanently affixed to restore complete bite strength.

Key Benefits of Dental Implants & Restorative Dentistry

  • 100% natural bite strength — eat steak, apples, and nuts with complete confidence
  • Stimulates jawbone density, preventing bone resorption and premature facial aging
  • Does not require grinding down or damaging adjacent healthy teeth (unlike bridges)
  • Permanent lifespan (98%+ success rate) with standard brushing and flossing

Frequently Asked Questions

Most patients report less discomfort than a routine tooth extraction. Local anesthesia completely numbs the area, and mild soreness afterwards resolves within 48 hours.

With standard daily brushing, flossing, and regular cleanings, dental implants are designed to last a lifetime.

Most adults in good general health with adequate jawbone density are ideal candidates. For patients with bone loss, gentle bone grafting is readily available.
